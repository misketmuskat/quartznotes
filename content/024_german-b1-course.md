## Course Description
- **Instructors:** Kathrin Kurz
- **Course Contents**:  
	Students' solid basic knowledge of German will be expanded on and improved in this course.  
	To obtain the UNIcert I certificate in German, you need the courses German Basic Course IV/V as well as Discussing and Grammar completed with a grade AND the UNIcert I examination. The cumulative acquisition (without additional examination) is possible with German Basic Course I, II/III, IV/V as well as Discussing and Grammar, all completed with a grade.
- **Further Grading Information**:  
	Requirement for course completion: *two written exams*
