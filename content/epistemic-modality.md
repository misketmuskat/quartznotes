#### Definition
Refers to expressions that communicate the *degree of certainty, knowledge, or belief about a statement.* It concerns what is known, believed, or considered probable. For example, "It might rain tonight" reflects uncertainty about the weather, indicating epistemic modality.
#### Etymology
The term "epistemic" comes from _epistēmē_ (meaning "knowledge" or "science" in Greek), so epistemic modality reflects knowledge or belief about the truth of something.
