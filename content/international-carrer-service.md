- #### UN
	- https://www.unjobnet.org/
	- UN Careers — https://careers.un.org
	- **Education:** A university degree, often a Master's, is required for many positions.  
	- **Work experience:** Work experience requirements vary by position, but many jobs require several years of relevant experience.  
	- **Languages:**  Excellent knowledge of English or French is essential, knowledge of other UN languages is an advantage. In your case German.   
	- **Personal skills:** The UN places a high value on integrity, analytical thinking, openness, trustworthiness, flexibility and adaptability.  
	- **Nationality:** Many UN organisations prefer applicants from their member states, but some accept applicants from all countries.  
	- **Age limit:** Some programmes, such as th as the UN YPP, have age limits. 
	- **Other requirements:** Depending on the position, specific expertise may be required, such as in a particular field or in the use of specific software.  
	- **Additional information:**
		- **YPP:** The UN Young Professional Programme (YPP) offers young graduates with good degrees an opportunity to start at the UN.  
		- **UN Volunteers:** UN Volunteers (UNV) offers opportunities for civilian experts in peace operations.  
		- **Job opportunities:** The UN offers a variety of job opportunities ranging from internships to staff positions.  
		- **Selection process:** The selection process at the UN is usually very demanding and competitive.  
		- **UN Secretariat Consultants:** There is also the opportunity to work as a UN Secretariat Advisor, which is another option for a UN career.
