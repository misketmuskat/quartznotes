### **İmamoğlu’s Social Media Usage: Pre- and Post-Arrest**
#### **Pre-Arrest Social Media Usage**
- **Strategy**: As Istanbul’s Mayor, İmamoğlu used social media to promote municipal services, emphasize transparency, and build direct connections with the public. His 2019 election campaign leveraged social media’s dialogic potential to foster two-way communication with voters (). Posts typically focused on projects, public engagement, and a positive leadership image.
- **Visual Usage**: Visuals aligned with Klemm’s “Visiotype” included “people-friendly” images (e.g., meetings with citizens) and “competence” images (e.g., official meetings, international events). For instance, his website ([www.ekremimamoglu.com](http://www.ekremimamoglu.com)) highlighted projects with the slogan “We Promised, We Delivered” ().
- **Tone and Message**: Posts were optimistic, constructive, and service-oriented, emphasizing values like “public service” and “transparency” through coordinated visuals and texts.
#### **Post-Arrest Social Media Usage**
- **Shift in Strategy**: Following his detention on March 19, 2025, and arrest on March 23, 2025, on corruption charges, İmamoğlu’s social media usage shifted to a more defiant, resistance-focused, and emotional tone. The ban on his X account on May 8, 2025 (,), and broader social media restrictions () meant that posts by supporters or the CHP gained prominence.
- **Visual and Textual Changes**:
    - **Resistance and Victimhood**: Post-arrest posts portrayed İmamoğlu as a “victimized leader” and “symbol of public will.” For example, a June 19, 2025, X post stated, “You fear my image, you fear my voice… but you cannot erase me from the hearts of this nation” (,). This reflects new image acts like “showing resistance” and “fostering solidarity.”
    - **Supporter-Driven Content**: With İmamoğlu’s X account banned, supporters used his image as profile pictures in a solidarity campaign (), amplifying his visual presence as a resistance symbol.
- **Contextual Impact**: Restrictions on social media () and his CHP presidential candidacy () shaped a narrative of political persecution, influencing the tone of posts.
