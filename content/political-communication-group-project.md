##### Soziale Semiotik
- **Representational Function:** What's in the image? What does it represent?
- **Interactional Function:** How does it reach us? Direct eye contact? Looking from above?
- **Compositional Function:** Perspective, color selection, vectors, focal point? How is the image structred?
##### Visual Framing
What story the images tell? How they direct the viewer?
- Values, identities, relationships that are highlighted in the visual
- Is there a distinction between "us" and "other"?
- Does the visual depict conflict, peace, or hope?
##### Inszenierungsmuster
Consistent, recurring themes and clichés in self-representation.
- “Am Rednerpult” (güç ve otorite)
- “Mit Kindern/Familie” (empati, yumuşaklık)
- “Sportlich unterwegs” (enerjik, genç, sağlıklı)
- “Bad in der Menge” (halkın içinden, popüler)
Personal branding of the politician.
##### Authentizitätsrhetorik
- Home scenes without makeup (e.g. AOC’s stories)
- Family time
- Humor, emotions, showing personal vulnerabilities
- Spontaneous, not formal images (e.g. drinking coffee or feeding her child)

### Bildlinguistik
A picture is a tool that not only “shows” but also communicates.
- **Pragmatik** — What meanings it carries?
- **Semantik** — What functions it has in what contexts?
- **Rhetorik** — What is the effect that is aimed?
