- **Project Name:** Discourse Analysis in Controversial Periods of Turkey
- **Dataset:** [Grand National Assembly of Turkish Parliament Transcripts 1920-2015](https://tulap.cmpe.boun.edu.tr/entities/dataset/84b30ee2-c748-4a6d-8bde-bff2ae4f9f82/full)
- **Research Objectives:**
	The linguistic and rhetorical shifts in parliamentary speeches during and around critical historical periods, such as the *1960 and 1980 military coups in Turkey*.
	- Turkish military memorandums: 1971, 1979, 1997, 2007.
- **Questions:**
	- How political narratives adapt to periods of crisis?
	- The *influence of the military on politics* in Turkey's parliamentary history.
	- How language reflects power dynamics, social tensions.
	- How the contexts of words such as *"democracy,” “constitution," and "army,"* *"security"* changed before, during, and after the period.
	- What concepts come to the fore in the conversations before and after the coup?
- **Methodology:**
	- **Pre-Coup:**
		- For example, 1957-1960 for the 1960 coup.
		- 1977-1980 for the 1980 coup.
	- **Post-Coup:**
		- For example, 1961-1965 after the 1960 coup.
		- 1983-1987 after the 1980 coup.
