## Research Possibilities 
All the references can be found in paper: Jan Blommaert & Chris Bulcaen, Critical Discourse Analysis, 2000
- **Political Discourse:** Wodak (1989), Chilton et al (1998), Fairclough (1989, 1992a), Fairclough & Mauranen (1997)
- **Ideology:** Hodge & Kress (1979), van Dijk (1998)
- **Racism:** van Dijk (1987, 1991, 1993b)
- **Economic Discourse:** Fairclough (1995b)
- **Advertisement and Promotional Culture:** Fairclough (1989, 1995b), Slembrouck (1993), Thornborrow (1998)
- **Media Language:** Fairclough (1995a), van Dijk (1991), Kress (1994)
- **Education:** Education is seen as a major area for the reproduction of social relations, including representation and identity formation. Kress (1997), Chouliaraki (1998)
